      How it works

      Anyone can add music or pause. The host resumes playback. In a two-person room, either listener can skip; larger rooms use a majority vote when a guest asks to skip.

      The host can loop the current track, repeat the queue, or shuffle upcoming music. A single remaining track repeats until another is added.

      “Wait for me” pauses everyone's music. Choose “I'm ready” when you're set; the host resumes after everyone is ready. “Let my ad finish” plays only your audio while the room waits.

      Volume is just for you. sc jams is independent of SoundCloud; playback and availability are provided by SoundCloud.
